# A-Share Domain Reference

Use this as a domain checklist, not as a substitute for current exchange rules. Verify current rules, market calendars, and corporate events when they affect a recommendation.

## Market Structure

Common A-share analysis dimensions:

- Broad indices: Shanghai Composite, Shenzhen Component, ChiNext, STAR-related indices, CSI 300, CSI 500, CSI 1000.
- Boards and styles: main board, ChiNext, STAR Market, Beijing Stock Exchange, ST and risk-warning stocks.
- Trading behavior: daily limit-up/limit-down structure, turnover, auction behavior, sector rotation, thematic speculation.
- Participants: retail flow, institutional flow, northbound flow if available, financing and securities lending where relevant.

## Common Theme Categories

- Policy-driven: industrial policy, fiscal support, reform, state-owned enterprise themes.
- Technology: AI, semiconductors, robotics, software, telecom, data infrastructure.
- Manufacturing: EV supply chain, machinery, automation, materials.
- Consumption: liquor, tourism, retail, healthcare services.
- Cyclical: coal, steel, nonferrous metals, chemicals, shipping.
- Defensive: banks, utilities, high dividend, telecom operators.

## Analysis Warnings

- A strong theme can still be late-cycle if leaders accelerate while followers weaken.
- One-day turnover spikes are not enough; look for continuity and breadth.
- Limit-up count without sector structure can be noise.
- Good fundamentals do not automatically create a good entry.
- Technical strength without catalyst freshness can reverse quickly.
