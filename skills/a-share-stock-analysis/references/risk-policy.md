# Risk Policy

Use this policy whenever producing market, stock, or portfolio suggestions.

## Required Separation

Separate:

- facts: verified data
- interpretation: what the data may mean
- uncertainty: missing or conflicting evidence
- action: conditional suggestion
- risk control: invalidation, sizing, review trigger

## Suggestion Language

Prefer:

- "可以观察"
- "满足 X 后再考虑"
- "跌破/失效于 Y 时降低仓位"
- "证据不足，暂不建议追高"
- "适合小仓位试错，不适合作为核心仓"

Avoid:

- "必涨"
- "稳赚"
- "无脑买"
- "满仓"
- "一定反弹"

## Risk Controls

Every suggestion should include at least two:

- position size or exposure range
- price/structure invalidation condition
- time-based review point
- catalyst invalidation
- data freshness requirement
- maximum drawdown threshold if user provided one

## Portfolio Risk

Check:

- single-stock concentration
- sector/theme concentration
- correlated catalysts
- liquidity risk
- event risk
- stale thesis risk
