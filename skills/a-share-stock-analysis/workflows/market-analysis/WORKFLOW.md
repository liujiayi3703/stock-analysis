# Market Analysis Workflow

Use this for broad A-share market condition, index trend, sentiment, liquidity, and risk appetite analysis.

## Steps

1. Validate index, breadth, turnover, sector, and macro/event data.
2. Analyze index structure: trend, support/resistance, volatility, and volume confirmation.
3. Analyze breadth: advancers/decliners, limit-up/down, new highs/lows if available.
4. Analyze liquidity: total turnover, northbound or institutional flow if available, financing balance when relevant.
5. Analyze sector rotation and hotspot concentration.
6. Identify market regime: risk-on, structural, defensive, high-volatility, or weak.
7. Translate regime into portfolio posture ranges, not absolute certainty.

## Market Regime Guide

| Regime | Evidence Pattern | Posture |
|---|---|---|
| Risk-on | rising index, expanding breadth, increasing turnover, multiple themes | higher exposure allowed with stops |
| Structural | index mixed, a few strong themes, weak broad participation | focus on leaders, avoid weak followers |
| Defensive | low turnover, defensive sectors lead, weak growth themes | reduce chase behavior |
| High-volatility | large intraday swings, hot themes rotate too fast | smaller position size, faster validation |
| Weak | falling index, poor breadth, failed rebounds | preserve cash, wait for confirmation |

## Output

Return:

- market regime
- evidence table
- dominant themes
- risk appetite
- exposure suggestion range
- invalidation conditions
