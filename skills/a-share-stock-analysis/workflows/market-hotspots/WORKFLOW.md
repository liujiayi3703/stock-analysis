# Market Hotspots Workflow

Use this to discover A-share sector themes, market main lines, short-term hotspots, and rotation signals.

## Steps

1. Validate market breadth, sector, and stock leader data.
2. Rank candidate hotspots by multiple signals, not one headline.
3. Separate durable main lines from one-day noise.
4. Identify leader stocks, followers, laggards, and failed breakouts.
5. Check catalysts: policy, earnings, industry news, price action, liquidity, and event calendar.
6. Look for contradiction: high turnover without follow-through, weak breadth, late-cycle acceleration, or leader divergence.
7. Produce a hotspot map and next-day watch conditions.

## Signal Stack

| Signal | Stronger Evidence |
|---|---|
| Breadth | many constituents advancing, low reliance on one mega-cap |
| Turnover | sector amount rising versus recent baseline |
| Limit-up structure | leaders seal early, followers expand, failed boards remain limited |
| Continuity | theme persists across sessions and survives index pullbacks |
| Catalyst | source-verifiable policy, earnings, demand, supply, or event driver |
| Institutional logic | fundamentals or valuation support the price action |

## Output

Use `templates/daily-market-report.md` for the hotspot section.

For each hotspot:

- theme name
- evidence
- leader stocks
- catalyst
- continuation condition
- invalidation condition
- risk level
