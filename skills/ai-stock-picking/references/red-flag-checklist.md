# Red Flag Checklist

Use this before allowing any company into the final shortlist.

## Business Red Flags

- no verified orders or revenue linkage
- only concept exposure, no material business exposure
- declining gross margin in a supposedly hot sector
- customer concentration not acknowledged
- inventory or receivables rising faster than revenue

## Financial Red Flags

- operating cash flow persistently negative
- high leverage without stable cash generation
- profit driven mainly by non-recurring gains
- frequent equity dilution without growth delivery
- auditor, disclosure, or governance concerns

## Market Red Flags

- one-day theme spike without follow-through
- leaders weakening while followers chase
- volume blow-off with no new evidence
- stock price already reflects extreme optimism
- sector index weak while the stock is hyped

## Decision

Any severe red flag changes the verdict to exclude or watch-only unless the user explicitly requests a speculative thesis.
