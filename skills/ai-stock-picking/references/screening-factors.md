# Screening Factors

Use these as reusable factors when creating or validating a stock pool.

## Hard Exclusions

- ST or delisting-risk status
- major unresolved governance risk
- severe financial fraud concern
- continuous losses without credible turnaround evidence
- liquidity too weak for the user's style
- thesis depends only on rumor or concept label

## Quantitative Factors

| Factor | Preferred Signal |
|---|---|
| Revenue growth | positive and accelerating versus peers |
| Net profit growth | positive and not only from non-recurring gains |
| ROE | stable or improving, better than sector average |
| Gross margin | high or improving, not collapsing under competition |
| Operating cash flow | positive or improving, consistent with profit |
| Asset-liability ratio | acceptable for the business model |
| Valuation | reasonable relative to growth, TAM, and quality |
| Volume | mild expansion or confirmed breakout, not pure speculation |

## Three-High Definition

- High growth: revenue/profit/order growth has evidence.
- High margin: gross/net margin is high, improving, or resilient.
- High bargaining power: pricing power, scarcity, customer stickiness, cost advantage, regulation barrier, brand, or technology moat.
