# Source-Backed Research Checklist

Use this checklist to build a ticker research report from evidence instead of narrative.

## Source Inventory

Prefer primary or data-backed sources:

- company filings, exchange disclosures, and investor relations materials
- official financial statements and segment data
- broker fundamentals or financial data terminals
- analyst consensus, target-price distribution, and estimate revisions
- ownership, insider activity, and strategic shareholder data
- multi-year historical financials for valuation ranges
- industry reports and supply-chain evidence

For A-share names, replace US-specific sources with exchange filings, annual/interim reports, exchange inquiry letters, major shareholder pledge data, industry association data, and trusted market databases.

## Data Table

Capture:

- current price, market cap, shares outstanding, free float
- revenue, gross margin, operating margin, net profit, ROE
- operating cash flow versus net profit
- debt ratio, interest burden, and short-term liquidity
- historical P/S, P/E, EV/EBITDA, or industry-relevant multiples
- analyst consensus and target range when available
- next earnings date or material event date

## Scenario Valuation

Use transparent formulas:

```text
future revenue = current revenue base * (1 + growth assumption)
target market value = target multiple * future revenue
target price = target market value / latest shares outstanding
```

For earnings-based companies, substitute EPS and P/E. For asset-heavy companies, consider P/B and ROE. For sum-of-the-parts work, value each segment separately.

Always disclose:

- revenue or earnings base period
- growth assumption and source
- target multiple and reason
- share count source and date
- sensitivity to dilution and multiple compression

## Risk Controls

Separate:

- operational stop: price/volatility based, used for trading discipline
- fundamental downside: conservative valuation if the thesis fails

Do not confuse a technical stop with fundamental fair value.

## 12-Point Red-Flag Review

1. Operating cash flow versus net profit
2. Gross margin versus peers
3. Advances, payables, and bargaining power
4. Capital expenditure efficiency
5. Management guidance history
6. Core team stability
7. Order quality: locked amount versus framework agreement
8. Customer concentration
9. Major shareholder pledge or forced-sale risk
10. Industry penetration stage
11. Related-party transaction frequency
12. Channel inventory authenticity

## Decision Rule

If the research cannot produce a target range, downside boundary, catalyst, and invalidation condition, keep the stock as "research incomplete" rather than action-ready.
