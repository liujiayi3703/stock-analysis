# Stock Picking Principles

Core principle: AI is an intelligence assistant and super analyst. The user owns strategy and final judgment; the agent performs data screening, evidence collection, and structured validation.

## Sector First

Do not begin with K-lines. Start with:

1. where macro, policy, and earnings direction align
2. which industry-chain links capture profit
3. which listed companies have real exposure and financial elasticity

## Company Quality

Prioritize companies with:

- high growth
- high or improving margins
- high bargaining power, moat, or supply-demand advantage
- positive or improving operating cash flow
- manageable leverage

## Validation Mindset

Every thesis needs:

- evidence
- contradiction
- missing data
- falsification condition
- review trigger

## Human-in-the-Loop

The agent may produce a shortlist, but the user decides whether it fits their trading system, risk tolerance, position size, and market timing.
