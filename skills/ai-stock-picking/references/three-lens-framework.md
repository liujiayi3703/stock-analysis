# Three-Lens Framework

This framework compresses stock-picking into three non-overlapping questions:

1. Macro lens: is the environment worth taking risk in?
2. Industry-chain lens: is this sector or company structurally important?
3. Execution lens: is the current setup actionable with defined risk?

Use the lenses sequentially. A later lens cannot rescue a failed earlier lens; it can only refine timing and sizing.

## Macro Lens

Decision:

- Tailwind: liquidity, policy, earnings, and sentiment support risk-taking.
- Headwind: policy, liquidity, geopolitics, or risk appetite argue for smaller size or no action.
- Wait: signals conflict or a known event is imminent.

Evidence to check:

- central-bank cycle, rates, yields, credit conditions
- fiscal, industrial, or regulatory policy direction
- market breadth, index trend, volatility, and capital flows
- event calendar: CPI, FOMC, earnings, key policy meetings

## Industry-Chain Lens

Decision:

- Strong: the company sits in a hard-to-replace node with real demand and limited supply response.
- Mixed: the company benefits from the theme but lacks pricing power, margins, orders, or scarcity.
- Weak: the story is mostly concept exposure without evidence.

Evidence to check:

- upstream/midstream/downstream map
- number of credible suppliers and substitution routes
- capacity expansion cycle and bottleneck duration
- order quality, customer concentration, and channel inventory
- whether the market has already fully priced the narrative

## Execution Lens

Decision:

- Actionable: trend, volume, volatility, and risk controls align.
- Wait: thesis is valid but price-volume confirmation is absent.
- Avoid: setup is crowded, unstable, or violates risk limits.

Evidence to check:

- market-day type: trend, range, reversal, event, or mixed
- volume confirmation, ATR/volatility expansion, support/resistance
- initial stop, trailing stop, and partial take-profit plan
- position size capped by maximum loss, not by conviction alone

## Conflict Rules

| Conflict | Default Handling |
|---|---|
| Macro strong, chain weak | Do not force a ticker. Keep scanning sectors. |
| Chain strong, macro weak | Reduce sizing; wait for macro relief or price discount. |
| Macro and chain strong, execution weak | Put on watchlist; define trigger. |
| Execution strong, evidence weak | Treat as trade-only momentum, not investment thesis. |

## Required Boundary

Every output must state missing data, invalidation, and whether the conclusion is suitable for observation, deep dive, watchlist, conditional trial, hold, reduce, or avoid.
