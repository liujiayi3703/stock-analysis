# Industry Chain Map Workflow

Use this after a sector has been selected.

## Steps

1. Break the sector into upstream, midstream, downstream, infrastructure, services, and distribution.
2. Identify profit pools:
   - highest gross margin
   - fastest revenue or order growth
   - strongest pricing power
   - lowest substitution risk
   - best supply-demand tightness
3. Map listed A-share companies to each link.
4. Mark each link as core beneficiary, secondary beneficiary, cyclical beneficiary, or concept follower.
5. Choose the best links for company screening.

## Chain Table

| Link | Representative products/services | Profit pool | Pricing power | Growth driver | Listed companies | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Handoff

Return:

- industry-chain map
- best links to investigate
- candidate tickers per link
- reasons to exclude weak links
