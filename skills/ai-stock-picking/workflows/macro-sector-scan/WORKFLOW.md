# Macro Sector Scan Workflow

Use this to find sectors worth watching over the next 6-12 months.

## Steps

1. Define horizon, market scope, and risk preference.
2. Gather current evidence from three lenses:
   - economic cycle: demand, inventory, price cycle, credit, consumption, exports
   - policy direction: official policy, regulation, fiscal support, strategic sectors
   - earnings growth: forecast revisions, reported growth, margin expansion, order visibility
3. Score each candidate sector using `references/sector-cycle-framework.md`.
4. Reject sectors driven only by slogans, social-media heat, or one-day price moves.
5. Select 3-7 sectors for industry-chain mapping.
