# Technical Assist Workflow

Use this after fundamental validation. Technical analysis is secondary evidence, not the core thesis.

## Steps

1. Validate OHLCV and adjustment status.
2. Identify trend state:
   - uptrend, range, downtrend, rebound, breakdown, or overextended
3. Check price-volume relationship:
   - mild accumulation
   - high-volume breakout
   - failed breakout
   - shrinking-volume pullback
   - abnormal speculative spike
4. Mark support, resistance, and invalidation zones.
5. Use MACD, RSI, moving averages, and relative strength as supporting signals.
6. Use `gf-dma-health-index` when the user asks for trend/valuation health or DMA confirmation.

## Output

| Ticker | Trend | Volume signal | Support | Resistance | Indicator notes | Technical verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Guardrail

Do not upgrade a weak fundamental thesis purely because the chart looks strong.
