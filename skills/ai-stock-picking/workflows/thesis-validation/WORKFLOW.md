# Thesis Validation Workflow

Use this to separate real alpha from concept chasing.

## Steps

1. Restate the sector and company thesis in one sentence.
2. List the evidence chain:
   - policy/cycle/news
   - demand change
   - industry-chain profit pool
   - company exposure
   - financial statement impact
   - market pricing and technical confirmation
3. Identify what would falsify the thesis.
4. Check red flags:
   - no orders
   - no revenue/profit linkage
   - no cash-flow support
   - margin deterioration
   - high leverage
   - pure theme hype
   - weak sector breadth
5. Use `serenity-alpha` when the thesis starts from news or a catalyst.

## Output

| Ticker | Thesis | Evidence chain complete? | Main missing link | Falsification point | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Verdicts

- validate
- watch for data
- reject as weak evidence
- reject as fake narrative
