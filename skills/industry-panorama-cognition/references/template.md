
## Analogy Rules

Use analogies only to clarify, not to replace evidence.

- Upstream resource/material: use a mine, granary, or water-source analogy. Explain cycles and bargaining power.
- Midstream manufacturing/equipment: use a craftsperson, mill, bridge, or engine analogy. Explain yield, scale, cost, delivery, and certification.
- Downstream brand/channel/application: use a shop, stage, restaurant, or stall analogy. Explain demand, brand, traffic, and customer stickiness.
- Platform/infrastructure: use a road, market, port, or operating-system analogy. Explain network effects, standards, and switching cost.
- R&D/innovative technology: use an alchemist, magic key, or seed-lab analogy. Explain uncertainty, validation, and commercialization.
